WHO WE ARE

WELCOME TO DOBU

DoBu Martial Arts is a martial arts and fitness centre designed for people of all experience levels.

Whether you are completely new to martial arts, returning to training or an experienced practitioner, DoBu provides a supportive environment where you can develop your skills and improve your fitness.

Our training programme includes Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, Karate and Muay Thai, alongside fitness and personal training services.

We also provide children's martial arts sessions, private tuition and specialist training options.
